An open question we do not paper over
-------------------------------------

The licence above settles the font. It does not, by itself, settle the
provenance of the particular binaries we fetch. Two facts, recorded so
that whoever reads this next does not have to rediscover them:

1. The repository we fetch from states, in its own LICENSE.md, that
   "Redistribution, modification, or commercial use of the font files
   without explicit permission from the original rights holders is not
   permitted" — a stricter term than KFGQPC's own. We therefore fetch
   from it and do not mirror it.

2. The files carry no licence string in their `name` table (field 13 is
   empty); they carry only "King Fahad Complex, All rights reserved",
   and their version string reads "FontCreator 11.0.0.2388" — i.e. they
   were produced with a font editor rather than issued by the Complex in
   this exact form.

Neither fact stops the app from working or from crediting the Complex.
Both are reasons to obtain the files from KFGQPC directly (or with their
written permission) before mirroring them anywhere.
